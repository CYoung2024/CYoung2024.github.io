NAS For My Parents

Jan 06, 2026

My parents have a ton a photos, and my dad is tired of using cloud storage. He doesn't like paying for it, and he doesn't like the idea of depending on Apple for all the storage. Mom's phone is constantly full, and she wants a better way of managing and deleting photos she doesn't want. With a bunch of free time after breaking my collarbone before Christmas, I decided to set up a NAS for them.

I don't want to spend much time maintaining it, and I want my dad to be able to handle the backup process so he feels comfortable. So, I spent way too much money and picked up a UGREEN NASync DXP2800 and 2 8TB HDDs.

Setup

Setting up the NAS was way too easy. It came with a good web interface and account setup process out of the box. Now if only I could get the family to use a shared password manager... I installed Docker and Immich and uploaeded a few photots as a test. All was well!

Pain

Then it was time to gather all the photos. I struggled immensely with our old Mac running macOS Sierra with a 2.5 GHz i5 and 4GB of 1333 MHz DDR3 RAM. There were so many photos stored in so many locations, and copying each of them onto an external drive took hours. Finder and the Photos app are so retarded that I had to resort to just copying ~/Documents, ~/Movies, ~/Pictures, and ~/Desktop from the terminal. Once I had them on the external drive, I plugged it into my laptop and uploaded everything to Immich.

Dad pulled out a dozen random SD cards, two old laptops, and an old MacBook. I ripped the photos from each and uploaded to immich.

Next was iCloud.
